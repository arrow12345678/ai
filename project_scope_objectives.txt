The project will encompass the following key stages:

2.  **Model Development:**

### Key Performance Objectives

The success of this project will be measured against the following key performance indicators (KPIs). These objectives are initial targets and may be subject to review and adjustment based on the available dataset, computational resources, and model performance during development.

1.  **License Plate Detection (LPD) Accuracy:**
    *   **Target:** >= 95%
    *   **Metric:** Percentage of license plates correctly detected in test images. A detection is considered correct if the Intersection over Union (IoU) between the predicted bounding box and the ground truth bounding box is >= 0.5.

2.  **Character Recognition Accuracy (CRR) / Per-Character Accuracy:**
    *   **Target:** >= 90%
    *   **Metric:** Percentage of individual characters correctly recognized from the segmented license plates. This is calculated as: `(Total Correctly Recognized Characters) / (Total Ground Truth Characters on Detected Plates)`.

3.  **Overall System Accuracy (Full Plate Recognition):**
    *   **Target:** >= 85%
    *   **Metric:** Percentage of license plates for which the entire alphanumeric text is correctly recognized. This means both the plate was correctly detected, and all its characters were correctly identified in the correct order.

**Note on Objectives:**
The stated performance objectives are ambitious targets. Actual achievable accuracy will depend significantly on the quality and diversity of the training data, the complexity of the license plate designs, environmental factors in the test images, and the chosen model architectures. These objectives will be continuously evaluated and may be refined as the project progresses.
